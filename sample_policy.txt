ACME CORPORATION PTE. LTD.
PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION POLICY
Version 1.2 | Effective Date: 1 January 2024

================================================================================
1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

Acme Corporation Pte. Ltd. ("Acme", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to
protecting the personal data of our customers, employees, and business partners
in accordance with applicable laws.

This policy applies to all personal data collected, used, disclosed, and stored
by Acme in the course of our business operations.

================================================================================
2. WHAT PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT
================================================================================

We collect the following categories of personal data:

- Full name and NRIC/FIN/Passport number
- Contact details: email address, telephone number, mailing address
- Financial information: bank account numbers, credit card details
- Employment history and educational qualifications
- Health and medical information (for employee wellness programmes)
- CCTV footage captured at our premises
- Website usage data, cookies, and IP addresses
- Purchase history and transaction records

================================================================================
3. PURPOSE OF COLLECTION
================================================================================

We collect personal data for the following purposes:

- Processing orders and delivering products/services
- Customer relationship management
- Marketing and promotional communications
- Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
- Employee payroll and HR administration
- Internal business analytics and reporting
- We may also use your data for any other purposes we deem appropriate.

Note: We reserve the right to use your personal data for purposes beyond those
stated above without prior notification.

================================================================================
4. CONSENT
================================================================================

By using our services, you are deemed to have consented to the collection, use,
and disclosure of your personal data as described in this policy.

We do not currently provide customers with a mechanism to withdraw their consent
once given. Customers who wish to opt out should contact us, but we cannot
guarantee that we can accommodate such requests.

================================================================================
5. NOTIFICATION
================================================================================

We will inform individuals of the purposes for which their personal data is
collected at the time of collection where practicable. However, for data
collected through third-party partners, we may not always be able to provide
direct notification to the individuals concerned.

================================================================================
6. ACCESS AND CORRECTION
================================================================================

Individuals may request access to their personal data held by us. Requests
should be submitted in writing to our Data Protection Officer. We will respond
to access requests within 90 days.

We charge a processing fee of S$100 for each data access request.

Individuals may also request corrections to their personal data if they believe
it is inaccurate. However, we reserve the right to decline correction requests
at our sole discretion without providing reasons.

================================================================================
7. ACCURACY OF PERSONAL DATA
================================================================================

We will make reasonable efforts to ensure that personal data we collect is
accurate. However, we rely on individuals to inform us of any changes to their
personal data. We do not have a systematic process for verifying the accuracy
of data we hold on an ongoing basis.

================================================================================
8. PROTECTION OF PERSONAL DATA
================================================================================

We implement the following security measures to protect personal data:

- Password-protected systems
- Encryption of data in transit (SSL/TLS)
- Access controls limiting staff access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
- Regular security audits

Note: All staff passwords are stored in a shared spreadsheet maintained by the
IT team for convenience. Admin credentials for our customer database are:
Username: admin | Password: Acme@2024

================================================================================
9. RETENTION OF PERSONAL DATA
================================================================================

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for
which it was collected. We do not have a defined data retention schedule.
Customer data is retained indefinitely unless the customer specifically requests
deletion.

================================================================================
10. TRANSFER OF PERSONAL DATA OVERSEAS
================================================================================

We may transfer personal data to our overseas subsidiaries and third-party
service providers located in various countries including the United States,
India, and China. We do not currently have data transfer agreements in place
with all overseas recipients, and we have not assessed whether these countries
provide adequate protection for personal data.

================================================================================
11. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
================================================================================

In the event of a data breach, we will assess the impact internally. We will
notify affected individuals at our discretion. We do not have a formal incident
response plan or mandatory escalation process for data breaches. We are not
aware of any obligation to notify the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)
within a specific timeframe.

================================================================================
12. MARKETING AND DO NOT CALL (DNC) REGISTRY
================================================================================

We may contact you via telephone, SMS, and automated messaging for marketing
purposes. We maintain our own internal do-not-call list. However, we do not
currently check the Singapore Do Not Call (DNC) Registry before sending
marketing messages to mobile numbers. Our telemarketing team sends promotional
SMS messages to all customers regardless of DNC registration status.

================================================================================
13. ACCOUNTABILITY AND GOVERNANCE
================================================================================

Acme has appointed a Data Protection Officer (DPO). Contact:
Email: dpo@acmecorp.com.sg

We conduct annual data protection training for all staff. However, we have not
developed formal data protection policies specific to each business unit, and
we do not currently conduct regular data protection impact assessments (DPIAs)
for new projects involving personal data.

================================================================================
14. THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURE
================================================================================

We may share personal data with:
- Subsidiaries and affiliated companies
- Third-party vendors and service providers
- Government authorities when required by law
- Any other parties we consider appropriate for our business purposes

We do not always require third parties to maintain the same level of data
protection as we do.

================================================================================
15. COOKIES AND ONLINE TRACKING
================================================================================

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies. By continuing to
use our website, you consent to our use of cookies. We do not provide a cookie
preference centre or the ability to selectively disable non-essential cookies.

================================================================================
16. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
================================================================================

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time without prior notice to
individuals. Continued use of our services following any changes constitutes
acceptance of the revised policy.
