PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION POLICY
Sunshine Community Services Pte. Ltd.
UEN: 202300001A
Effective Date: 1 January 2025
Last Reviewed: 1 January 2025

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This Policy explains how Sunshine Community Services Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") 
collects, uses, discloses and protects your personal data in accordance with the 
Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

2. PURPOSE LIMITATION
We collect personal data only for the following specific purposes:
(a) Processing membership applications and renewals
(b) Communicating programme schedules and updates
(c) Processing payments for services rendered
(d) Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
We will not use your personal data for any purpose beyond those stated above 
without obtaining your prior consent.

3. NOTIFICATION
We will notify you of the purposes for which your personal data is collected 
at or before the time of collection, without exception.

4. CONSENT
We obtain your explicit written consent before collecting, using or disclosing 
your personal data. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting our DPO 
at dpo@sunshine.org.sg. Withdrawal of consent will be processed within 5 
business days. We will inform you of the consequences of withdrawal.

5. ACCESS AND CORRECTION
You may request access to or correction of your personal data at any time.
We will respond to all requests within 10 business days (well within the 
statutory 30-day limit). A nominal administrative fee of S$5 applies for 
access requests. We will provide clear reasons for any refusal of correction 
requests, and you may appeal any such decision.

6. ACCURACY
We take reasonable steps to ensure your personal data is accurate and complete.
We conduct quarterly data verification exercises and encourage you to update 
your information at any time via our member portal.

7. PROTECTION
We implement the following security measures to protect your personal data:
(a) AES-256 encryption for data at rest and in transit
(b) Role-based access controls with least-privilege principles
(c) Multi-factor authentication for all staff accounts
(d) Annual third-party penetration testing
(e) All vendors are contractually bound to PDPA-equivalent standards via DPAs
(f) Staff undergo mandatory annual data protection training

8. RETENTION LIMITATION
We retain personal data only for as long as necessary:
- Membership records: 7 years after membership ends (for legal compliance)
- Programme attendance: 2 years
- Payment records: 7 years (IRAS requirement)
All data is securely deleted or anonymised upon expiry of retention periods.

9. TRANSFER LIMITATION
We do not transfer personal data outside Singapore. Where transfers are 
necessary for operational purposes, we ensure recipient organisations are 
bound by legally enforceable obligations providing PDPA-comparable protection,
including signed data transfer agreements.

10. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION
We have a documented Incident Response Plan (IRP-2025-001). In the event of 
a data breach:
(a) We will assess the breach within 24 hours
(b) We will notify the PDPC within 3 calendar days if the breach is notifiable
(c) We will notify affected individuals without undue delay where significant 
    harm is likely
(d) All incidents are logged in our breach register

11. ACCOUNTABILITY
Our Data Protection Officer is Mr. Tan Wei Ming (dpo@sunshine.org.sg, 
+65 6123 4567). We conduct annual DPIAs for all new projects involving 
personal data. Business unit-specific data protection guidelines are 
maintained and reviewed annually.

12. DO NOT CALL (DNC) REGISTRY
We check the Singapore DNC Registry before sending any marketing messages.
We only send marketing communications to individuals who have provided 
explicit consent. You may opt out of marketing communications at any time 
by contacting us or clicking unsubscribe.
